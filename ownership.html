Who recalls America

Every FDA recall since 2012, rolled up from 9,054 filing entities to the corporate parents that actually own them. Philips files under 20 names. Medtronic under 29. Then each parent is joined to the federal contracts it holds — the same government that classifies the recall is buying from the company that filed it.

Sources. Recalls: openFDA enforcement reports (food, drug, device), June 2012 – August 2026, US federal public records. Federal contracts: USAspending.gov, contract obligations 2012–2026, matched by recipient name.

Method and limits. Parent mapping is a curated regex over normalized filing names and includes known acquisitions (Hospira→Pfizer, Merge→GE, Biomet→Zimmer, Bard→BD). It captures 60 parents covering 7,662 recalls; the remaining ~19,700 recalls belong to firms outside the mapping. Federal dollars are matched by name, not by UEI, so figures include all recipients whose name contains the parent string — treat them as order-of-magnitude, not audited. A recall is not evidence of wrongdoing, and holding a federal contract is not evidence of favouritism. Recalls are a cost to the companies that file them.
