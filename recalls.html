Sources. FDA food, drug and device enforcement reports via openFDA. Consumer products via CPSC SaferProducts. Vehicles via NHTSA. All are U.S. federal public records, queried live from your browser each time you load this page.

Meat, poultry and processed eggs are missing. They are regulated by USDA FSIS, not the FDA, and FSIS blocks automated requests from most networks. Check fsis.usda.gov/recalls directly for deli meat, ground beef and poultry.

Agency data is provided as-is and may be incomplete or delayed — do not use this site for decisions about food safety, medication or medical care. Authoritative sources: fda.gov, cpsc.gov, nhtsa.gov.
